你说了八遍，是同一句话

八次尝试拆开看，三个格子里填的东西没变过。问题不在词汇量， 在你把中文逐词查表、再按中文语序拼装。

前七行是同一道题。第八行是从没练过的新题——三个格子照样全中， 所以要纠的不是某一句的错，是默认的拼装方式。

三个症状

1名词硬塞进形容词位和动词位

中文说「XX 的 YY 太高」，你就写 the YY is high。cost 是名词，形容词位要 expensive，要用 cost 就让它当动词 costs too much。

  • is so high cost
  • has hard cost
  • is so very cost
  • is high risk
  • I advice to do

2主语是名词堆，谓语只有 is / has

主语永远是「这个方案 / 迁移 / 改动」这类名词，动词永远是 is 或 has。名词堆越长，后面越接不上。

  • This solution for magration has high cost
  • this magration solution is high cost

3中文概念逐词直译，对方听不懂

这条比语法错严重得多——前面那些顶多让人觉得你英语一般，这个直接让沟通失败。

  • separate multiple stagings　←　分阶段
  • the gray test　←　灰度

破局动作 · 只有一个

开口前先定两件事：主语是谁，动词是什么。

主语换成人或动作，谓语换成真动词——名词堆自己就消失了。别记第二个动作，记两个等于没记。

主语动词说出来是这样
Wecan'tWe can't do this all at once.
Moving thiscostsMoving this costs too much.
IwantI want to break this into steps.

句库 · 开会前扫一眼

每句都不用抽象名词当表语，主语是人或动作。挑一句背，只挑一句——记三句等于没记。

这个方案的迁移成本太高，我倾向于分阶段做。在队列

  1. This would cost too much to move all at once. Let's do it in steps.
  2. Moving everything over is a big lift. I'd rather go piece by piece.
  3. We can't do this in one shot. Break it up and take one part at a time.

这个改动的风险太大了，我建议先灰度。在队列

  1. This change could break a lot. Let's roll it out to a few customers first.
  2. I don't want to ship this to everyone at once. Give it to a small group and watch.
  3. Too much can go wrong here. Try it on a slice of traffic first.

这个模块的复杂度已经超过我们能维护的程度了。在队列

  1. Nobody understands this module end to end any more.
  2. We've made this too complicated to keep up with.
  3. This has grown past what the team can hold in their heads.

这次排查的工作量比我预估的大三倍。在队列

  1. This took me three times longer than I said it would.
  2. I was way off on this one. It's three times the work.
  3. Tracking this down is eating far more time than I planned for.

这个告警的误报率太高，运维已经开始忽略它了。在队列

  1. This alert cries wolf so often that the on-call team tunes it out.
  2. We fire this when nothing is wrong, so nobody reads it any more.
  3. Ops stopped trusting this one. It goes off for no reason.

这个方案对现有部署的侵入性太强。在队列

  1. This asks customers to change too much of what they already run.
  2. We'd be reaching into parts of their setup we shouldn't touch.
  3. Rolling this out means tearing up what they already have.

这个漏洞的利用门槛很低，必须这周修。在队列

  1. Anyone could pull this off. We need it fixed this week.
  2. It takes almost no skill to abuse this, so it can't wait.
  3. Someone could walk right through this. It goes out this week.

这块的技术债已经开始拖慢交付了。在队列

  1. We're paying for shortcuts we took, and it shows in how slow we ship.
  2. The mess we left behind is what's holding up releases.
  3. Every change here takes longer than it should because of what we skipped.

这个接口在高并发下响应时间会翻倍。在队列

  1. When a lot of people hit this at once, it takes twice as long to answer.
  2. Under load it slows down to half its speed.
  3. Push enough traffic at it and the wait doubles.

我们的检测覆盖率还不够，先补最关键的那几类。在队列

  1. We're missing too much. Let's cover the attacks that actually matter first.
  2. There are gaps in what we catch. Start with the ones that hurt.
  3. We don't see enough of what's out there. Fill the worst holes first.

这个性能问题的根因不在代码，在部署方式。在队列

  1. The code is fine. It's how we're running it.
  2. Nothing here is slow. The way we deploy it is.
  3. We're looking in the wrong place. This is a deployment problem.

划掉的唯一条件：在真实会议里说出来过一次。练多少遍都不算完成。
